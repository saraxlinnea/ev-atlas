Methods

How values enter the table, and where coverage remains incomplete. The longer record is docs/METHODOLOGY.md.

Claims

Each value begins as a claim: field, value, source, access date, and tier. Compare, Makes, and Figures read files generated from those claims. A wrong number is corrected in the claim, then regenerated. The CSV is not edited by hand.

When no qualifying source is found, the cell stays empty. Blank means not found, not zero. Primary and secondary claims require a concrete URL. Derived values require a formula and inputs.

Source tiers

  • P (primary): OEM page or press kit, EPA, NHTSA, or other government source.
  • S (secondary): Outlet, teardown, or aggregator. Ranked below P even when the figure looks solid.
  • D (derived): Computed from other claims, with formula and inputs recorded.
  • unverified: Plausible but not confirmed. Repetition across sites does not upgrade the tier.

When sources disagree, both claims remain and are marked as conflicts. Compare shows the winning value only (higher tier, then higher confidence). A quiet † marker flags the cell; activating it reveals the other recorded value and both sources. Equal tier and confidence keep the earlier claim as the shown value.

EPA and NHTSA

EPA: Per-vehicle records from fueleconomy.gov, checked against the bulk download. The EPA vehicle id appears in each claim's notes. Most rows use the entry-level EPA configuration; notes record which trim or wheel set was selected. Label kWh/100mi is wall-side (includes charging losses) and is not the same as derived battery-side figures. EPA does not publish which range factor (5-cycle, 0.7, or approved) produced each label.

NHTSA recalls: Queried by make, model, and year, not trim. An HTTP 400 with count zero often means the model string did not match, not that no campaigns exist. Zero-recall results are checked against NHTSA's recall flat file.

Known limitations

As of 2026-10-05. Coverage is incomplete; this is not a ranking and not full OEM disclosure for all vehicles.

Reading the table

  • Rows are trim-specific. Sister trims can differ on range, pack, and price.
  • Make dossiers list only vehicles already in EV Atlas, keyed by make (Chevrolet ≠ Cadillac). Spec summaries use filled winners only (min–max or stated on N of M). A make-identity Background layer (claims/makes/) adds HQ, optional parent, brand type, EV focus, home market, listing when the make is the issuer, and a number-free summary from opened OEM/IR pages; blank means not found. Not company encyclopedias, rankings, or full OEM catalogs.
  • region is the spec market (US or CN), not brand HQ. Compare Market defaults to US. EPA columns are US-cycle; CLTC columns are China-cycle; blank EPA on a CN row is expected.
  • CLTC values are never stored in EPA fields, and the atlas does not invent a CLTC↔EPA conversion or cross-cycle ranking. EPA Figures omit non-US rows; a separate CLTC range vs pack figure covers China-market rows only. Neither is a ranking.
  • China list prices are sourced in CNY, then Compare shows USD via a documented Federal Reserve H.10 / FRED DEXCHUS YTD average (not a full-year average; not a US MSRP). NHTSA recall fields stay blank on China-only rows (not applied, not zero).
  • China-market densification (2026-10-03) plus CN add (2026-10-04): Compare shows USD MSRP only (msrp_cny remains the FX input; same Fed H.10 / FRED DEXCHUS 6.8228 mean). Eight CN rows when Market includes CN. BYD Seal buyer fields remain aggregator S (byd.com / MIIT still non-extractable). New entry rows: BYD Han EV 506KM 尊贵型, Zeekr 7X 后驱智驾版 75 kWh, NIO New ES6 75 kWh. Port still blank where no page named GB/T or 国标充电口. Full per-vehicle notes in docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
  • US critical buyer densify (2026-10-04): Ariya FWD 63 kWh and ZDX RWD pack/MSRP/charging from adjacent-year OEM press; Wagoneer S Launch Edition MSRP; CCS1 for i4, Countryman, IONIQ 6, Sierra EV.
  • US buyer densify + accel display honesty (2026-10-04): onboard AC filled for R1T 11.5 kW, R2 11 kW, Ioniq 9 11 kW, Prologue 11.5 kW, ZDX 11.5 kW; Polestar 2 port CCS1; Prologue port corrected to CCS1. Blank counts among 42 US rows: MSRP 3→3, port 3→2, peak DC 6→6, onboard AC 8→3, pack 1→1, accel 16 blank unchanged (no new OEM 0-60 mph). Accel column subtitle is cycle in cell; cells label (0–100 km/h) from claim basis (no mph conversion). Still blank: Polestar/Daytona/Lightning MSRP; several peak DC and port gaps; Wagoneer/Model S/Model Y onboard AC; Silverado pack; sixteen US 0-60 blanks.
  • Secondary sources may appear where primary OEM text was unavailable; they rank below primary.
  • Pack-based derived metrics require a gross or usable pack basis, not unstated.
  • OEM peak DC kW and 0-60 times are manufacturer claims under conditions that are often underspecified.
  • MSRP is the stated base for the configured trim; destination, tax, and options vary.
  • body.width_in is without-mirrors / body width only. With-mirrors-only figures are withheld.

Tesla, Lucid, and Rivian

  • Live tesla.com often blocks scripted fetch. Model 3 and Model S OEM figures commonly use Wayback snapshots, which can lag the live page.
  • Recorded conflicts include Model 3 MSRP and Model S MSRP and curb weight. Tesla torque and most Tesla onboard AC stay blank (OEM silent). Model 3 width uses a without-mirrors secondary figure; Model S width stays blank when only with-mirrors figures exist. Tesla chemistry stays blank.
  • Lucid live pages advance model years early. MY2026 Air Pure and Grand Touring claims draw on press and technical PDFs; chemistry stays blank (900V architecture is not chemistry). Some Gravity specs cite 2027-labeled pages with lowered confidence.
  • Rivian Support battery specifications (re-opened 2026-10-05) restore Dual Standard LFP and 315 V minimum pack, and add Dual Large NCA / 282.5 V on the Dual Large row. R2 chemistry and voltage stay blank.

Chemistry, voltage, and robotaxi stubs

  • Heatmap after the 2026-10-05 densify: chemistry 24/55 and system voltage 24/55 (was 18/50 each on the pre-pass flatten). Fills only from opened sources; blanks mean not found.
  • Waymo Ojai and Zoox Robotaxi are sparse fleet / AV stubs, not retail MSRP or EPA Compare peers. EPA, NHTSA, and MSRP stay blank unless a matching retail or government record is opened. Full notes in docs/METHODOLOGY.md.

OEM coverage

  • Most vehicles have at least partial OEM or newsroom buyer fields from fetchable pages. Compare-column densify leaves torque on 34/39, onboard AC on 31/39, and body width on 34/39.
  • BMW i4 eDrive35 is the atlas trim, but BMW USA no longer lists eDrive35 for 2026 (eDrive40 and above). No remapping was invented.
  • Ariya FWD 63 kWh and ZDX RWD now carry adjacent-year OEM buyer fills (model_year 2025 / 2024); onboard AC and 0-60 on ZDX, and Ariya 0-60, stay blank where OEM text did not support the atlas trim.
  • Honda Prologue Supercharger access is described via a NACS-CCS adapter; the vehicle port is recorded as CCS. Prologue onboard AC kW still blank on fetchable Honda pages.
  • Leaf and Bolt 0-60 were not stated on the fetchable OEM pages used here.
