Methods

How values enter the table, and where coverage remains incomplete. The longer record is docs/METHODOLOGY.md.

Claims

Each value begins as a claim: field, value, source, access date, and tier. Compare and Figures read a file generated from those claims. A wrong number is corrected in the claim, then regenerated. The CSV is not edited by hand.

When no qualifying source is found, the cell stays empty. Blank means not found, not zero. Primary and secondary claims require a concrete URL. Derived values require a formula and inputs.

Source tiers

  • P (primary): OEM page or press kit, EPA, NHTSA, or other government source.
  • S (secondary): Outlet, teardown, or aggregator. Ranked below P even when the figure looks solid.
  • D (derived): Computed from other claims, with formula and inputs recorded.
  • unverified: Plausible but not confirmed. Repetition across sites does not upgrade the tier.

When sources disagree, both claims remain and are marked as conflicts. Compare shows the winning value only (higher tier, then higher confidence). A quiet † marker flags the cell; activating it reveals the other recorded value and both sources. Equal tier and confidence keep the earlier claim as the shown value.

EPA and NHTSA

EPA: Per-vehicle records from fueleconomy.gov, checked against the bulk download. The EPA vehicle id appears in each claim's notes. Most rows use the entry-level EPA configuration; notes record which trim or wheel set was selected. Label kWh/100mi is wall-side (includes charging losses) and is not the same as derived battery-side figures. EPA does not publish which range factor (5-cycle, 0.7, or approved) produced each label.

NHTSA recalls: Queried by make, model, and year, not trim. An HTTP 400 with count zero often means the model string did not match, not that no campaigns exist. Zero-recall results are checked against NHTSA's recall flat file.

Known limitations

As of 2026-10-03. Coverage is incomplete; this is not a ranking and not full OEM disclosure for all vehicles.

Reading the table

  • Rows are trim-specific. Sister trims can differ on range, pack, and price.
  • region is the spec market (US or CN), not brand HQ. Compare Market defaults to US. EPA columns are US-cycle; CLTC columns are China-cycle; blank EPA on a CN row is expected.
  • CLTC values are never stored in EPA fields, and the atlas does not invent a CLTC↔EPA conversion or cross-cycle ranking. EPA Figures omit non-US rows.
  • China list prices use CNY when sourced; no invented CNY→USD FX. NHTSA recall fields stay blank on China-only rows (not applied, not zero).
  • Secondary sources may appear where primary OEM text was unavailable; they rank below primary.
  • Pack-based derived metrics require a gross or usable pack basis, not unstated.
  • OEM peak DC kW and 0-60 times are manufacturer claims under conditions that are often underspecified.
  • MSRP is the stated base for the configured trim; destination, tax, and options vary.
  • body.width_in is without-mirrors / body width only. With-mirrors-only figures are withheld.

Tesla and Lucid

  • Live tesla.com often blocks scripted fetch. Model 3 and Model S OEM figures commonly use Wayback snapshots, which can lag the live page.
  • Recorded conflicts include Model 3 MSRP and Model S MSRP and curb weight. Tesla torque and most Tesla onboard AC stay blank (OEM silent). Model 3 width uses a without-mirrors secondary figure; Model S width stays blank when only with-mirrors figures exist.
  • Lucid live pages advance model years early. MY2026 Air Pure claims draw on press and technical PDFs; some Gravity specs cite 2027-labeled pages with lowered confidence.

OEM coverage

  • Most vehicles have at least partial OEM or newsroom buyer fields from fetchable pages. Compare-column densify leaves torque on 34/39, onboard AC on 31/39, and body width on 34/39.
  • BMW i4 eDrive35 is the atlas trim, but BMW USA no longer lists eDrive35 for 2026 (eDrive40 and above). No remapping was invented.
  • Ariya FWD 63 kWh and ZDX RWD stay EPA/NHTSA-thin (discontinued US pages; wrong-year or wrong-drive buyer figures not applied).
  • Honda Prologue Supercharger access is described via a NACS-CCS adapter; the vehicle port is recorded as CCS. Prologue onboard AC kW still blank on fetchable Honda pages.
  • Leaf and Bolt 0-60 were not stated on the fetchable OEM pages used here.

Model years and availability

  • Most entries are MY2026. Rivian R2, Chevrolet Bolt, and BMW iX3 are MY2027; Hyundai Ioniq 6, Polestar 2, VW ID. Buzz, and F-150 Lightning SR are MY2025.
  • Sale status (2026-10-03): current for Bolt LT, Leaf 75 kWh, IONIQ 5 SE RWD Standard Range, Prologue Single Motor FWD, Mach-E Select RWD standard range, and EV6 Light RWD (atlas Standard Range RWD). discontinued for Ariya and ZDX. The other 31 remain unverified_availability. Confirmation URLs are listed in docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
  • BMW iX3 MY2027 has no recall campaign count yet: API unmatched-string HTTP 400, flat file has no BMW MY2027 or IX3 rows, and products lists no iX3 for MY2027. Blank, not a fake zero.
  • NHTSA products vs campaign conflicts are recorded for some MY2026 nameplates; both views are kept.
